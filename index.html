Privacy Policy

HiddenDrop does not collect personal information. The only data we store is your crypto wallet address (which you provide voluntarily) to send prizes. We do not share your data with third parties. Payments are processed by NOWPayments. Game state is stored on Supabase.

Security

All payments are processed through NOWPayments, a trusted crypto payment processor. We never store your private keys or payment details. Prize payouts are sent directly to the wallet address you provide. Tile state is recorded on Supabase with real-time verification.

How it Works

1. Choose a category and select a mystery image.

2. Pay $2 USDT to reveal a random tile. The image stays blurred the whole time — no matter how many tiles are revealed!

3. The player who reveals the LAST tile wins 20% of the total prize pool, sent instantly to their wallet.

4. After the last tile, a new round begins with a fresh image!
