How It Works

1. Choose a category and select a mystery image from the thumbnails above.

2. Deposit funds — minimum $15 (5 tiles at $3 each). Your balance is saved to your wallet address.

3. Reveal tiles from your balance. The image stays fully blurred the entire time — no peeking!

4. Last tile wins $100! The player who reveals the final tile wins $100 USDT, sent manually to their wallet within 24 hours.

5. Share your referral link — earn a free tile every time a friend makes their first deposit!

Privacy Policy

HiddenDrop does not collect personal information. The only data stored is your crypto wallet address (provided voluntarily) to track your balance and send winnings. We do not share your data with third parties. Payments are processed by NOWPayments. Game state is stored securely on Supabase.

Security

All payments are processed through NOWPayments, a trusted non-custodial crypto payment processor. We never store your private keys. Prize payouts go directly to the wallet address you provide. Your balance is stored on Supabase with real-time verification. Each payment generates a unique address — we never reuse addresses.
